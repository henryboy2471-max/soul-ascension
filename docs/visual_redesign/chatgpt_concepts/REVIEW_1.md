## Concept A (home) vs the brief
- PLAY EPISODE is the dominant gold button; episode card, RESUME state, secondary nav: all meet the brief.
- Both PLAY EPISODE and RESUME EPISODE 1 are shown together. The game has no restart, so "PLAY EPISODE" next to RESUME is ambiguous. Proposal: fresh save = PLAY EPISODE only; saved progress = RESUME EPISODE 1 as the gold primary, no second play button.
- The hero (home art and card) is the superseded white-haired Echo, not Echo V2 (black locs, gold beads, red/black). Must be replaced or labelled temporary.
- Energy (60/120, the mission currency) and level are missing from the top bar; mail (with badge) and wifi icons imply online features the offline, no-account game does not have. Summon/Shop/Events/Battle Pass are shown as normal items; they are not available and need a SOON state.
- New logotype (ornate SOUL): the approved identity needs an owner decision before it replaces the current wordmark. New card copy ("uncover the truth behind the Soul Realm Breach") is new story text; needs approval.
- Small text (nav labels, card blurb) will be about 8-9 px on a 667 px-wide phone landscape: too small.

## Concept B (gameplay) vs the brief
- Strong: layered skyline, elevated rail, lit storefronts (RAMEN kept), wet reflections, mist, lamp posts, tram station.
- It is one flattened illustration. Integration needs separate plates (sky / far / mid / near / ground / foreground) with consistent lighting; it cannot be sliced reliably from a flat image.
- It shows only the first screen of a 2500 px world. Relay (x 1560), mural (1860) and breach (2200) have no landmarks yet.
- Story mood conflict: the story is a stalled tram network in an Aether storm (SERVICE HALTED, rain); the concept shows a lively, lit station with a crowd and no rain.
- The painted crowd competes with the readable NPCs (Mira, Hero); background people should be low-contrast silhouettes or removed (the game adds its own pedestrians). Benches and posts at lane height imply blockers; collision is only Mira.
- Mira and the hero drawn here do not match the game's Mira design (violet cape, gold band) or Echo V2; they are placeholders.
- Both strips are about 3.5:1, not the game's 16:9 (1280x720), so scale and HUD fit cannot be verified from this image.
