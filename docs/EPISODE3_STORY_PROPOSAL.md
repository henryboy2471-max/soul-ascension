# Episode 3 - "The Relay Keeper" - story and new characters (PROPOSAL for owner approval; nothing is implemented)

Season 1 stays at exactly 10 episodes; this is episode 3 of 10, the first of six "array" episodes (Rain Array 7 fell in Episode 2; **six arrays remain**, so Episodes 3-8 each silence one). Direction: adventure first - roughly three quarters of the episode is exploration, conversation, a routing puzzle, a stealth stretch and a cinematic; combat is the Soul Realm boss at the end.
Technical plan and checklist: `docs/EPISODE3_PREP.md`. Season context: `docs/SEASON1_PLAN.md`.

## Premise
It is the morning after Vaust's Resonance Amnesty. Registration booths have appeared in every tram station, and the stalled trams (stopped since the night of Episode 1) are suddenly the city's choke point: the Lantern Circuit can't move families or supplies, and Meridian can "process" anyone who reaches a station. The Circuit's old tram dispatcher, the **Relay Keeper**, has vanished from the Central Relay - and Array 6 is hidden inside it. Echo and Mira must find him, bring the trams back on the Circuit's terms, and silence the array.

## Story beats
1. **Cold open (cinematic, ~40 s).** Dawn over Skybridge 09; every tram board flips to a countdown "REGISTRATION 46:12:09". Echo wakes in the Circuit safehouse; Pell's radio crackles; Mira lays the map on the table: the Central Relay controls every line.
2. **Skybridge Concourse (exploration hub).** Stranded passengers, a registration booth with a queue, a ticket clerk, a child with a lantern, a closed newsstand with a hand-written Circuit notice. Conversations give three leads to the Keeper (a brass tag, a maintenance log, a passenger's memory). Echo can stay unseen by keeping out of the booth drone's sight lines (light stealth).
3. **Platform 3 workshop.** The Keeper's hideout, abandoned in a hurry: tools, a half-built analog relay, a photo of a younger Mira. Optional collectibles (brass tags) fill the Codex.
4. **Meeting the Keeper (dialogue with choices).** He is in the signal tower above the yard, armed with a flare gun and distrust: "You're why the rain sings." Depending on Echo's answers he opens up (or makes Echo earn it by fixing the tower lamp). He reveals the key fact: **the arrays don't just broadcast - they listen.** Each one reports a "reading" of the Echo signal to a tower, and the Relay keeps the only mechanical log.
5. **Restore the lines (puzzle + traversal).** Three track junctions across the yard must be set so a rescue tram can run from the depot to the relay; scanners sweep the yard (stealth stretches between junctions). Each junction repair triggers a short tram cinematic shot.
6. **The Relay core.** Array 6 sits inside the central relay; Echo syncs it and the breach opens: **"THE LAST TRAIN"** Soul Realm - a looping rail-scape under a violet sky.
7. **Boss: the Relay Warden.** Wave 1 a Resonance Shade; boss the Warden (rail-sweep telegraphs). At 50% **"FULL SERVICE"**: every junction in the arena lights and the sweeps double. Support moment: the Keeper's whistle (from the tower, over radio) halts one sweep, like Mira's lantern in Episode 2.
8. **Aftermath (cinematic + exploration).** The trams run; families board; the Keeper gives Echo a brass **Tram Pass** (a story item that unlocks quick travel between connected zones in later episodes). In the yard a sealed cargo car marked **ECHO** rolls past on its own.
9. **Cliffhanger.** Mira forces the car's seal: inside, a dormant resonance coil tuned to Echo's exact frequency and stamped with the Quiet Index sigil. "They didn't just listen to you. They built a copy." The car pulls out of the yard toward the Spire district. NEXT EPISODE: A Debt to Lightning (Episode 4), FIVE ARRAYS REMAIN.

## Sample dialogue (tone check; final script comes after approval)

> **ECHO:** Afraid?
> **TEO:** Seven of them. Each one listens, then reports. I kept the only log that isn't digital. Paper doesn't get overwritten, kid.

> **TEO:** I did. I sent it by being missing. Worked on you, didn't it?
