# Season 1 - "The Fracture" - exactly 10 episodes (planning document)

Status: planning only. Episodes 1 and 2 are implemented and approved. Episodes 3-10 are proposals built on the established story (`docs/STORY_AND_ROADMAP.md`, `scripts/story/episode_data.gd`, `scripts/story/episode2_data.gd`, `docs/EPISODE2_PLAN.md`). Tags: **[established]** = already in the game or an approved design doc; **[proposed]** = new detail awaiting owner approval. Season 1 stays at exactly 10 episodes; ideas beyond that go to Season 2 (`Crown of Frequencies`) in the backlog.

## Season arc (connected storyline)
The Echo signal (the protagonist) opened a Soul Realm in Skybridge 09 and answered a "second signal" from beyond the sky. Meridian answers with control: Director Vaust's Resonance Amnesty (register or be suppressed). The violet rain of Episode 2 came from **Rain Array 7**, one of **seven** Meridian arrays tuned to Echo's signature [established: "Seven means six more"]. Episodes 3-8 follow the six remaining arrays and the people each one is hurting; Episode 9 takes the broadcast that drives them; Episode 10 confronts Vaust and reveals where the second signal really comes from. Mira's Lantern Circuit is the heart of the story; Sable Irix (archivist) and the Quiet Index are introduced as the hidden hand behind Meridian's tuning [from the design doc's Season 1 summary].

Each episode follows the same adventure shape: arrival and exploration -> NPC conversations that change the objective -> a non-combat mission (repair/escort/stealth/rhythm/puzzle) -> the Soul Realm breach -> a boss or major encounter -> aftermath and cliffhanger. Numbers for rewards are proposals and scale with the progression plan (roadmap slice D).

## Episode details

### Episode 1 - A Spark in the Static [established, implemented]
- Arc: a quiet worker's Echo signal flares on a storm night; Meridian scanners sweep the bridges.
- Main characters: Echo (player), Mira Vey, the Soul-Warped Enforcer.
- Locations: Skybridge 09 (tram platform, tram board, mural, relay terminal), the Soul Realm breach.
- Objectives: find Mira -> hold the relay terminal -> enter the breach -> survive the Shade and the Enforcer.
- NPCs: Mira (portrait dialogue), board and mural lore.
- Revelations: Echo learns from others' resonance; a second signal answers from "impossible" distance.
- Encounter: Soul-Warped Enforcer (Phase 2 at 50%).
- Rewards: 120 XP / 250 gold, Codex (Mira, Meridian Notice, Lantern Circuit, Echo Signal, Soul Realm, Enforcer).
- Cliffhanger: "Meridian will come looking for you now." Next-episode card: Under the Violet Rain.

### Episode 2 - Under the Violet Rain [established, implemented]
- Arc: violet rain carries the Echo signal; sleepers sleepwalk; Echo calms the quarter and silences Rain Array 7.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Pell (radio medic), the sleepers, the Hollow Cantor, Director Vaust (broadcast).
- Locations: Lantern Quarter (Depot 4, terrace, roof), the Sleepers' Stair Soul Realm.
- Objectives: talk to Mira -> sync three lantern resonators (memories) -> open the roof gate -> Rain Array 7 -> breach -> Shade + Cantor.
- Revelations: the rain is tuned to Echo; "Seven means six more."
- Encounter: Hollow Cantor (THE CHORUS RISES at 50%; Mira's lantern support once).
- Rewards: 150 XP / 300 gold, Codex (Violet Rain, The Sleepers, Rain Array 7, Hollow Cantor).
- Cliffhanger: Vaust's 48-hour Resonance Amnesty; "1 ASCENDANT FLAGGED / SIGNATURE: ECHO." Next: The Relay Keeper, six arrays remain.

### Episode 3 - The Relay Keeper [outline from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: with the city's trams down, the Lantern Circuit cannot move people or supplies. The relay keeper who maintained the tram network is missing; Array 6 hides in the central relay.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, the Relay Keeper (new NPC, an elder Circuit technician), Relay Warden (mini-boss).
- Locations: Central Tram Relay hall, switchyard, a stalled tram line with signal towers.
- Objectives: find the keeper (follow tram maintenance clues) -> restore three track junctions (routing puzzle) -> reach the relay core -> silence Array 6.
- NPC interactions: the keeper's story (his Meridian "retirement"); passengers stranded on a stalled tram (small side talks with branching answers).
- Revelations: the Arrays do not only broadcast, they listen: each one reports a "reading" back to a tower.
- Encounter: Relay Warden, a Meridian maintenance construct (track-sweep telegraphs; Phase 2 "FULL SERVICE").
- Rewards: 180 XP / 340 gold; new ability: Echo Dash (movement) used in later zones; Codex (Relay Keeper, Relay Warden, Array 6).
- Cliffhanger: the restored trams run, and one carries a sealed Meridian cargo labeled ECHO.

### Episode 4 - A Debt to Lightning [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: a community substation powering the Lantern Quarter is under attack by Meridian "disruptors"; Mira owes the substation crew a debt.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Substation foreman (new), Voltaic Disruptor.
- Locations: the substation yard, cable tunnels, rooftop transformer ring.
- Objectives: defend generators in lanes while repairing; escort a cable crew; Array 5 tuning mast.
- NPCs: foreman and crew with relationship choices (spend scarce power on a clinic or on defenses).
- Revelations: Mira's debt is to someone now on Meridian's list; arrays draw power from the same grid.
- Encounter: Disruptor waves + Voltaic Disruptor (lightning telegraphs).
- Rewards: 200 XP / 360 gold; skill-tree point; Codex (Substation, Disruptors, Array 5).
- Cliffhanger: Mira's brother, thought safe, appears on a Meridian informant list.

### Episode 5 - Glasshound Pursuit [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: a Meridian surveillance drone has tagged Echo; Echo and Mira must break the pursuit and take its Array 4 module.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Courier (new NPC who knows the rooftops), Glasshound (mini-boss).
- Locations: rooftop chase route, market arcades, a drone gantry.
- Objectives: stealth-lite escape (avoid scan beams), rhythm/timed rooftop crossings, lure the drone into the gantry.
- NPCs: the Courier teaches the rooftop route; market vendors hide Echo if the player earns trust.
- Revelations: the drone's logs reference "Level Zero", a facility under the city.
- Encounter: Glasshound (a lens-eyed hunter; cone-of-sight telegraphs).
- Rewards: 220 XP / 380 gold; accessory slot unlocked; Codex (Glasshound, Level Zero).
- Cliffhanger: the logs' coordinates open a maintenance hatch beneath the city.

### Episode 6 - A City Beneath the City [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: Echo enters the first laboratory, where Meridian built the arrays' prototype; Array 3 is the core of the lab.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Lab AI voice (new, SYSTEM), a guardian machine, a first glimpse of Tessel Nox's lab (hidden-boss thread, no fight).
- Locations: service tunnels, sensor maze, the prototype hall.
- Objectives: sensor-maze navigation with light/dark timing; restore power to doors; reach the prototype hall.
- NPCs: terminals and recordings; a trapped technician (conversation tree).
- Revelations: the arrays were first tested on a person; "discarded prototype" hints (Tessel Nox, hidden boss line).
- Encounter: guardian machine (major encounter, pattern puzzle boss).
- Rewards: 240 XP / 400 gold; Codex (Level Zero, Prototype Array, Tessel Nox [locked hint]).
- Cliffhanger: a sealed archive door unlocks to Echo's resonance; behind it, someone is still alive.

### Episode 7 - The Null Surgeon [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: Sable Irix, the archivist, helps recover suppressed Aether records; the Null Surgeon guards them.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Sable Irix (introduced), the Null Surgeon (mini-boss).
- Locations: the archive vault, record stacks, an operating theatre.
- Objectives: recover three record sets (each requires a different non-combat skill: stealth, repair, conversation).
- NPCs: Sable (remembers discarded futures; mistrusts easy victories), archive caretaker.
- Revelations: the Quiet Index catalogs possible futures and removes unpredictable people; Array 2 was tuned from these records; Echo is "unpredictable".
- Encounter: Null Surgeon (suppression-field telegraphs that cancel abilities).
- Rewards: 260 XP / 420 gold; Sable joins as a support ally; Codex (Quiet Index, Sable Irix).
- Cliffhanger: the records contain a living subject's name: the Circuit's informant.

### Episode 8 - A Friend's Frequency [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: the informant who betrayed the safehouse is found and is in danger; Echo must rescue them while protecting the rest of the Circuit. Array 1 is the last array.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Sable, the informant (new NPC, a Circuit member who sold the safehouse to protect their family).
- Locations: a Meridian holding block, the informant's family apartment, the Array 1 tower.
- Objectives: split rescue (two parallel objectives with a timer): extract the informant and shelter their family; silence Array 1.
- NPCs: the informant and family (a moral choice affects dialogue and a small reward).
- Revelations: the informant gave up only a name Meridian already had: Vaust is hunting something older than Echo.
- Encounter: no boss; a pressure sequence; the hard choice is the encounter.
- Rewards: 280 XP / 440 gold; skill-tree point; Codex (The Informant, Array 1).
- Cliffhanger: the informant reveals the broadcast tower that feeds every array.

### Episode 9 - Break the Broadcast [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: with the arrays silent, Vaust's amnesty broadcast keeps the city quiet through suppression pylons; Echo and allies take them down at once.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Sable, Pell, Lantern Circuit volunteers, the Pylon Choir (major encounter).
- Locations: three pylon districts linked by trams (reuse of Episode 3 trams), the broadcast spire base.
- Objectives: coordinate three simultaneous pylon assaults (switch between allies; multi-lane defense).
- NPCs: every earlier episode's side characters return (callbacks), conversation choices decide who helps.
- Revelations: the broadcast has a carrier that is not Meridian's: it contains the second signal.
- Encounter: Pylon Choir (an echo of the Cantor motif at larger scale, optional callback fight).
- Rewards: 300 XP / 480 gold; ally abilities; Codex (Suppression Pylons, Carrier).
- Cliffhanger: Vaust sends a personal invitation to Echo: "Come to the Spire. Alone."

### Episode 10 - Director Vaust [from design doc; details proposed]
- Arc: Echo confronts Vaust, who believes compulsory Aether control prevents another fracture; the Season's mystery of the second signal resolves.
- Characters: Echo, Mira, Sable, Director Vaust, a final cameo of every ally.
- Locations: Meridian Spire (atrium, control floor, observation roof).
- Objectives: ascend the Spire (puzzle floors tied to the arrays), a dialogue duel with choices, final Soul Realm confrontation.
- NPCs: Vaust (long cinematic conversation), allies holding the lower floors.
- Revelations: the signal originates beyond the sky and is not hostile: it is a call for help older than the fracture; defeating Vaust exposes it (matches the design doc's Season 1 summary).
- Encounter: Director Vaust, major boss in two phases (his control tools turn against him in Phase 2).
- Rewards: 400 XP / 600 gold; Season 1 completion reward; ending Codex; unlocks Season 2 teaser (Crown of Frequencies).
- Cliffhanger: the signal repeats, now addressed to a name other than Echo's. Season 2 "Crown of Frequencies" begins in the backlog (not part of the 10).

## Cross-episode threads
- Mira Vey: Lantern Circuit leader; her debt (Ep4) and brother (Ep4->8) drive her arc.
- Sable Irix: introduced Ep6-7, joins as ally Ep7, central in Ep9-10.
- Quiet Index: seeded Ep3 (cargo labels), revealed Ep7, present in the final act.
- Hidden thread: Tessel Nox (hidden boss), accessible after the side-mission chain (Lost Signal, Night Courier, The Last Tram, Borrowed Light, Names in the Rain), not counted as episodes.
- Progression: abilities and skill points are spread so that each episode unlocks something used immediately (see roadmap slice D).
